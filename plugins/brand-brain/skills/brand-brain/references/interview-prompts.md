# Interview prompts (use only for gaps)

## A. No visual identity yet: design-system prompt

Ask the owner what they sell and who it is for, then run this (yourself or in their Claude Project):

```
Act as a senior brand designer.
Build a complete design system for my business:
[what you sell + who it is for]

Include:
• A logo direction
• A colour palette with hex codes
• Heading and body fonts
• Buttons, cards and spacing
• Do and don't rules

Ask me 5 questions before you start.
```

Everything this produces is **proposed** until the owner approves it. Say so at the top of `01-brand-kit.md`.

## B. No written copy yet: voice interview (ask in one batch)

1. Describe your brand as if it were a person: three adjectives.
2. Paste 2–3 pieces of writing you like the sound of. Any brand counts.
3. Three words or phrases you'd happily use, and three you'd never be caught saying.
4. Formal or casual? Short or flowing? Humour: none, dry or playful?
5. British or American spelling? Emojis or hashtags?
6. Anything you must never claim (legal, medical, pricing)?

## C. No positioning yet: Brand DNA, line by line

Go through the six lines (who, problem, how, different, proof, one line) one at a time. Push back on vague answers ("everyone", "quality") and ask for a specific customer and a specific situation.
