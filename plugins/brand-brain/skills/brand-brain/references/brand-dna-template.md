# Brand DNA template → `03-brand-dna.md`

The six lines come first. They are the quickest test of whether the brand has been understood. Keep each one to a single sentence.

```markdown
# <Brand> Brand Brain — 03 Brand DNA

Source keys: ...

## The Brand DNA in six lines
1. **WHO WE HELP**: We help [customer] who [situation].
2. **THE PROBLEM**: They struggle with [problem].
3. **HOW WE SOLVE IT**: We [what you do] so they [result].
4. **WHY WE ARE DIFFERENT**: Unlike [others], we [difference].
5. **PROOF**: [numbers, results, reviews], verifiable ones only.
6. **ONE LINE**: [Brand] helps [customer] get [result].

| Segment | Situation | Problem | What they need to hear |

## 7. Proof

### Proof we don't have yet: do not claim
```
